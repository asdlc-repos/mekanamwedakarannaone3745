# Overview

An internal app for WSO2 employees to request time off and for managers to review and approve those requests.

# Personas

- Employee — requests leave and checks their balance.
- Manager — approves or rejects leave requests from their team.

# Features

- An employee selects dates and submits a leave request.
- Managers see pending requests from people they manage.
- A manager approves or rejects each request.
- Employees see the status of their submitted requests.
- Everyone can view their remaining leave balance.
